1. Mention the complications caused by Zika virus during pregnancy
	**Zika Virus - Complications in Pregnancy**
	
	- **Transmission:** Can occur from mother-to-child, commonly in the **first trimester**.
	- **Associated Syndrome:** Zika virus infection during pregnancy can lead to **Congenital Zika syndrome (CZS)**.
	- **Key Characteristics of CZS:** CZS is characterised by **microcephaly** and **other neurological manifestations**.
