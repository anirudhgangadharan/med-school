	**Morphology of _Ascaris lumbricoides_ - Fertilized Egg**
	
	- **General Description:** Fertilized eggs are a form in which _Ascaris_ exists. Female adult worms liberate fertilized eggs.
	- **Size & Shape:** **Round to oval**, measuring **45–75 μm × 35–50 μm**.
	- **Eggshell/Coat:** Surrounded by a **thick mamillated, albuminous coat**. This coat contributes to their high resistance, allowing them to survive for as long as 15 years, resistant to disinfectants. Decorticated eggs may lose this coat.
	- **Contents:** Contains a **large unsegmented ovum** of granular mass. There is a **clear crescentic space at both the poles**.
	- **Appearance (Saline Mount):** **Bile-stained**, appearing **golden brown**.
	- **Floatation:** **Floats in saturated salt solution**. This is in contrast to unfertilized eggs which do not float.
