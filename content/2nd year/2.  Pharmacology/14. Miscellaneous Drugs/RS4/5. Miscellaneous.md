1. Define Antimicrobial Stewardship programme
	#### **Definition:**
	
	A **coordinated, systematic programme** in healthcare settings designed to **optimize antimicrobial use** by:
	
	- Promoting **appropriate selection, dosing, route, and duration** of antimicrobial therapy.
	    
	- Ensuring **effective treatment** of infections.
	    
	- Minimizing **toxicity, resistance development, and healthcare costs**.
	    
	
	---
	
	#### **Core Objectives:**
	
	1. **Enhance clinical outcomes** → cure/prevention of infection.
	    
	2. **Reduce adverse drug effects** (toxicity, drug interactions).
	    
	3. **Limit antimicrobial resistance** (AMR).
	    
	4. **Lower healthcare expenditure** without compromising care.
	    
	
	---
	
	#### **Key Components:**
	
	- **Formulary restriction & pre-authorization** of specific antimicrobials.
	    
	- **Prospective audit with feedback** to prescribers.
	    
	- **Guidelines & protocols** (empirical vs definitive therapy).
	    
	- **De-escalation** of broad-spectrum to narrow-spectrum agents.
	    
	- **Dose optimization** (PK/PD-based).
	    
	- **IV-to-oral switch** when feasible.
	    
	- **Surveillance**: Resistance trends, antimicrobial use data.
	    
	- **Education & training** of healthcare professionals.
	    
	
	---
	
	#### **Clinical Relevance (Exam-Oriented):**
	
	- ASP directly addresses the **global threat of AMR** (multi-drug resistant organisms).
	    
	- Mandated/recommended by **WHO, CDC, IDSA** as an essential hospital programme.
	    
	- Integral to **rational antimicrobial therapy** (Tripathi) and **judicious antibiotic use** (Goodman & Gilman).
	    
	
	---
	
	### **Exam-Style Summary (High-Yield):**
	
	- **Definition:** ASP = structured programme to optimize antimicrobial use.
	    
	- **Goals:** Improve outcomes, minimize toxicity, reduce resistance, cut costs.
	    
	- **Strategies:** Formulary restriction, audit/feedback, de-escalation, PK/PD dosing, IV→oral switch, surveillance, education.
	    
	- **Clinical importance:** Key in combating **antimicrobial resistance**.
