1. Bulk purgatives/forming agents-name
	### **Bulk Purgatives / Forming Agents**
	
	**I. Names:**
	
	- **Bran**
	- **Methylcellulose**
	- **Agar**
	- **Ispaghula** (obtained from _Plantago ovata_)
	
	**II. Mechanism of Action:**
	
	- These are **indigestible, hydrophilic substances**.
	- They **absorb water and swell up**, thereby **increasing the bulk of stools**.
	- This increased bulk causes **mechanical distension**, which **stimulates peristalsis** and **promotes defaecation**.
	
	**III. Clinical Notes & Adverse Effects:**
	
	- **Onset of Action:** Takes **1–3 days** for the evacuation of formed stools.
	- **Administration:** **Large amounts of water must be taken** with these purgatives to prevent intestinal obstruction.
	- **Other Effects:**
	    - Dietary fibres (like pectin) can bind bile acids, increasing their excretion in faeces and **lowering plasma LDL**.
	- **Uses (Clinical Relevance):**
	    - Should be **encouraged in patients with Irritable Bowel Syndrome** (IBS).
	- **Contraindications/Precautions:**
	    - Should be **avoided in patients with megacolon or megarectum**.
	- **Adverse Effects:**
	    - **Abdominal discomfort**
	    - **Flatus**
