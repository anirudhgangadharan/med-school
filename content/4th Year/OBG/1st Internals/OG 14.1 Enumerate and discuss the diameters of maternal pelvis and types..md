### **1. Types of Maternal Pelvis (Parent Pelvic Types)**

On the basis of the shape of the inlet, the female pelvis is divided into four parent types. Commonly, intermediate forms with a combination of features (e.g., gyne-android) are found, but the pure forms are classified as follows:

- **Gynecoid Pelvis (50%):**
    - _Inlet Shape:_ Round.
    - _Anatomical Features:_ The sidewalls are straight, and the subpubic angle is wide (85°). The bituberous diameter is normal.
    - _Obstetric Outcome:_ Favorable. Engagement occurs with no difficulty, and easy anterior rotation is common, posing no difficulty during delivery.
- **Anthropoid Pelvis (25%):**
    - _Inlet Shape:_ Long oval.
    - _Anatomical Features:_ The sidewalls are straight, the subpubic angle is slightly narrow, and the bituberous diameter is normal or short.
    - _Obstetric Outcome:_ Usually presents no difficulty for engagement. Anterior rotation is common, and delivery poses no difficulty.
- **Android Pelvis (20%):**
    - _Inlet Shape:_ Triangular.
    - _Anatomical Features:_ The sidewalls are convergent, resulting in a narrow subpubic angle and a short bituberous diameter.
    - _Obstetric Outcome:_ Engagement is delayed, and flexion is defective. It frequently causes difficult anterior rotation, or non-rotation/arrest in the cavity. Delivery is difficult with an increased chance of perineal injuries.
- **Platypelloid Pelvis (5%):**
    - _Inlet Shape:_ Flat.
    - _Anatomical Features:_ The sidewalls are divergent, leading to a very wide subpubic angle (more than 90°) and a wide bituberous diameter.
    - _Obstetric Outcome:_ Engagement is difficult and occurs by an exaggerated parietal presentation. However, rotation and delivery face no difficulty once the inlet is negotiated.

### **2. Diameters of the Maternal Pelvis**

The true pelvis forms the bony canal through which the fetus passes and is divided into the inlet, cavity, and outlet.

**A. Diameters of the Pelvic Inlet (Brim)**

- **Anteroposterior Diameters:**
    - _True Conjugate (Anatomical Conjugate):_ Distance between the midpoint of the sacral promontory and the inner margin of the upper border of the symphysis pubis. It measures **11 cm**.
    - _Obstetric Conjugate:_ Distance between the midpoint of the sacral promontory and the prominent bony projection on the inner surface of the symphysis pubis. It is the shortest AP diameter of the inlet and measures **10 cm**.
    - _Diagonal Conjugate:_ Distance between the lower border of the symphysis pubis and the midpoint of the sacral promontory. It measures **12 cm** and is the only diameter that can be measured clinically.
- **Transverse Diameter:** The distance between the two farthest points on the pelvic brim over the iliopectineal lines. It measures **13 cm**.
- **Oblique Diameters:** Extends from one sacroiliac joint to the opposite iliopubic eminence. It measures **12 cm**.
- **Sacrocotyloid Diameter:** Distance between the midpoint of the sacral promontory and the iliopubic eminence. It measures **9.5 cm**.

**B. Diameters of the Pelvic Cavity** The cavity extends from the inlet to the plane of least pelvic dimensions.

- **Anteroposterior Diameter:** Measures from the midpoint on the posterior surface of the symphysis pubis to the junction of the 2nd and 3rd sacral vertebrae. It measures **12 cm**.
- **Transverse Diameter:** It measures **12 cm**, though it cannot be precisely measured clinically as the points lie over soft tissues covering the sacrosciatic notches and obturator foramina.

**C. Diameters of the Midpelvis** The midpelvis is bounded above by the plane of greatest pelvic dimensions and below by the midpelvic plane (level of ischial spines).

- **Anteroposterior Diameter:** Extends from the lower border of the symphysis pubis to the point on the sacrum where the midpelvic plane meets. It measures **11.5 cm**.
- **Transverse (Bispinous) Diameter:** Measures between the two ischial spines. It is **10.5 cm**.
- **Posterior Sagittal Diameter:** Extends from the midpoint of the bispinous diameter to the sacrum. It measures **4.5 cm**.

**D. Diameters of the Pelvic Outlet** The outlet is divided into the obstetrical outlet and the anatomical outlet.

- **Obstetrical Outlet (Plane of least pelvic dimensions):**
    
    - _Anteroposterior Diameter:_ Extends from the inferior border of the symphysis pubis to the tip of the sacrum. It measures **11 cm**.
    - _Transverse (Bispinous) Diameter:_ Distance between the tips of the two ischial spines. It measures **10.5 cm**.
    - _Posterior Sagittal Diameter:_ Distance between the tip of the sacrum and the midpoint of the bispinous diameter. It measures **5 cm**.
- **Anatomical Outlet (Bony outlet):**
    
    - _Anteroposterior Diameter:_ Extends from the lower border of the symphysis pubis to the tip of the coccyx. It measures **13 cm** (when the coccyx is pushed back during labor).
    - _Transverse Diameter of the Outlet (Intertuberous):_ Measures between the inner borders of the ischial tuberosities. It measures **11 cm**.
    - _Posterior Sagittal Diameter:_ Anteroposterior distance between the sacrococcygeal joint and the midpoint of the transverse diameter of the outlet. It measures **8.5 cm**.
