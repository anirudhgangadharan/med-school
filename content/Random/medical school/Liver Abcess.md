1. entamoeba histolytica (causing amoebic liver abscess) 
	1.  if it is amoebic entry --> via colon -->portal circulation -->liver -->liver abscess --> anchovy sauce because of mixing of pus with the blood
	2. clinical features -->
		1. symptoms - pain in the right upper abdominal quadrant , fever, pain maybe referred to the right should tip (referred pain), amoebic dysentery (as this organism enters through the colon)
		2. classically presented as - fever with chills, red and tender right upper abdomen, hepatomegaly
	3. investigations include ultrasound - USG to locate the liver abscess, and the normal routine blood investigations - we can see the elevation of CRP, Interleukins and cytokines
	4. Management -pharmacological management(via [[Metronidazole or Tinidazole]]) preferred before aspiration via guided USG or other non invasive methods.
	5. Complications - liver failure
