### MEDICO-SOCIAL DIAGNOSIS

Here is a 4-month-old male child who belongs to Class IV SES as per Modified BG Prasad Classification, with complaints of cough, fever, fast breathing and chest indrawing. The child is malnourished and there is inadequate ventilation in the house — diagnosed with **Pneumonia (Acute Respiratory Infection).**

### PREVENTION

**Individual Level:**

- **Primordial Prevention →**
    
    - Improve housing and living condition
    - Reduce indoor air pollution
    - Prevention of overcrowding
    - Socio-economic upliftment
- **Primary Prevention:**
    
    - Proper immunization
    - Exclusive breastfeeding
- **Secondary Prevention:**
    
    - Early recognition of ARI symptoms
    - Early diagnosis
    - Treatment
- **Tertiary Prevention:**
    
    - Oxygen therapy
    - Nutritional rehabilitation
    - Follow-up

**Family Level:**

- Maintain hygiene practices
- Avoid exposure to respiratory infections
- Adequate ventilation
- Ensure smoke-free home

**Community Level:**

- Universal Immunization Programme
- National Immunization Schedule
- IMNCI
- Integrated Global Action Plan for Prevention and Control of Pneumonia & Diarrhoea

---

### SUMMARY

Here is a 4-month-old male baby named **Shivaraj**, came to hospital with complaints of cough, fever, fast breathing, chest indrawing. The child belongs to Class IV a/c Modified BG Prasad Classification. The child is malnourished and lives in a house which has inadequate ventilation. On examination child uses accessory muscle for respiration, chest indrawing and decreased resonance — is diagnosed with **Pneumonia**.
