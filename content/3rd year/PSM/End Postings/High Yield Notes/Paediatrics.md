## National Immunization Schedule (India) 2025

|Age|Vaccine(s)|Dose (ml)|Route|Site|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**At Birth (0–24 hrs)**|BCG, OPV 0 (Polio), Hepatitis B (Hep B-0)|BCG: 0.05 ml|BCG: Intradermal (ID)|BCG: Left upper arm|
|||OPV: 2 drops|OPV: Oral|Oral administration|
|||Hep B: 0.5 ml|Hep B: Intramuscular (IM)|Left thigh|
|**6 Weeks**|DPT-1, IPV-1, Hep B-2, Hib-1, PCV-1, Rotavirus-1|0.5 ml each|IM (DPT, Hep B, Hib, PCV, IPV)|Anterolateral thigh / Upper arm|
|||Rotavirus: oral|Oral|Buccal mucosa|
|**10 Weeks**|DPT-2, IPV-2, Hep B-3, Hib-2, PCV-2, Rotavirus-2|0.5 ml each|IM (injections)|Anterolateral thigh / Upper arm|
|||Rotavirus: oral|Oral|Buccal mucosa|
|**14 Weeks**|DPT-3, IPV-3, Hib-3, PCV-3, Rotavirus-3|0.5 ml each|IM (injections)|Anterolateral thigh / Upper arm|
|||Rotavirus: oral|Oral|Buccal mucosa|
|**6 Months**|Hepatitis B-3|0.5 ml|IM|Left thigh|
|**9 Months**|Measles-Rubella (MR), Japanese Encephalitis (JE)*|0.5 ml|Subcutaneous (SC)|Upper arm (Left or Right)|
|||JE: 0.25 ml|IM (if JE)|Upper arm|
|**12 Months**|Hepatitis A (optional), Yellow Fever (if applicable)|0.5 ml|IM or SC|Upper arm|
|**15 Months**|MMR-2, Varicella, PCV Booster|0.5 ml each|Subcutaneous (MMR, Varicella)|Upper arm|
|||PCV Booster|IM|Anterolateral thigh/Upper arm|
|**18 Months**|DPT Booster-1, Hib Booster, IPV Booster|0.5 ml each|IM|Upper arm|
|**5 Years**|DPT Booster-2, OPV Booster|0.5 ml (DPT)|IM (DPT) / Oral (OPV)|Upper arm (DPT), Oral (OPV)|
|**10 Years (Adolescents)**|Td (Tetanus, Diphtheria)|0.5 ml|IM|Upper arm|
|**16 Years**|Td Booster|0.5 ml|IM|Upper arm|

*JE vaccine given only in endemic districts.

## Memory Tricks for Recall Under Pressure

- **At Birth:** "**HOB**" — _H_epatitis B, _O_PV (0 dose), _B_CG
    
- **6 Weeks:** "**DIPR RV**" — _D_PT 1, _I_PV1, _P_CV1, _R_otavirus 1 (also Hep B2, Hib 1)
    
- **10 Weeks:** Repeat "DIPR RV" (2nd doses)
    
- **14 Weeks:** "DIPR RV" (3rd doses)
    
- **9 Months:** "**MR J**" — _M_easles-_R_ubella and _J_apanese encephalitis
    
- **15 Months:** "**MVP**" — _M_MR 2nd dose, _V_aricella, _P_CV Booster
    
- **Boosters:** Remember "**DPT 18M, DPT 5Y, Td 10Y and 16Y**"
    

Additional mnemonic: **HOB DIPR RV MR J MVP DPT TD** to sequentially recall vaccines.

This systematic mnemonic aligns with age groups and vaccines, helping rapid recall during exams or practice.

---

For oral vaccines (OPV, Rotavirus, Cholera), remember "**O**" for Oral administration and for injected vaccines always note route (IM/SC) and injection sites.
