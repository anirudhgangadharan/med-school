1. Acute severe asthma - name the drugs used

**DRUGS USED IN ACUTE SEVERE ASTHMA**

Acute severe asthma (including life-threatening exacerbations and status asthmaticus) is a medical emergency requiring rapid pharmacological intervention. The key drugs used in its management are classified as follows:

**1. Oxygen**

- **Oxygen Inhalation:** Immediate and continuous administration to maintain oxygen saturation >95%,.

**2. Short-Acting $\beta_2$-Agonists (Bronchodilators)**

- **Salbutamol or Terbutaline (Inhaled):** Administered via nebulizer or Metered Dose Inhaler (MDI) with a spacer. Nebulization is repeated every 20 minutes for the first hour, and then the interval is gradually increased if the patient improves,.
- **Adrenaline or Terbutaline (Subcutaneous):** A subcutaneous injection of adrenaline or terbutaline is indicated in life-threatening asthma (e.g., silent chest, cyanosis, poor respiratory efforts),.

**3. Corticosteroids**

- **Hydrocortisone (Intravenous):** Administered as an injection (5 mg/kg) and continued every 6 to 8 hourly,. Table guidelines also suggest an initial IV dose of 8 to 10 mg/kg followed by maintenance doses.
- **Prednisolone (Oral):** An oral dose of 1–2 mg/kg is given immediately and continued once daily for 5–7 days without tapering,.

**4. Anticholinergics**

- **Ipratropium Bromide (Inhaled):** If there is no improvement with initial $\beta_2$-agonists, inhaled ipratropium ($250 \mu g$) is added and administered every 20 minutes.

**5. Methylxanthines**

- **Theophylline (Intravenous):** An injectable loading dose of theophylline followed by a continuous infusion is started if there is poor or no response to initial bronchodilator and steroid therapy,.
- **Aminophylline (Intravenous):** Used for status asthmaticus; given as a 5–7 mg/kg IV loading dose, followed by a continuous infusion at 0.5–1 mg/kg/hr.

**6. Adjunctive Therapy (For Refractory Cases)**

- **Magnesium Sulfate (Intravenous):** Given as a slow IV infusion ($50 mg/kg$ dissolved in dextrose over 30 minutes) if the patient does not improve with the above measures,. It acts through both anti-inflammatory and bronchodilator effects.
