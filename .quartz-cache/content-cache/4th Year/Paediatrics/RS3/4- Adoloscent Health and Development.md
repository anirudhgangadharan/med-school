1. Name the sexual maturity rating (SMR) staging in boys.
**SEXUAL MATURITY RATING (SMR) / TANNER'S STAGING IN BOYS**

Tanner's sexual maturity rating (SMR) quantifies pubertal sexual growth from stage 1 to 5. In boys, the assessment is primarily based on two parameters: **Genitalia (Penis, Testes, and Scrotum) development** and **Pubic Hair growth**. The objective tool typically used for measuring testicular size/volume is Prader’s orchidometer.

**SMR STAGE 1 (Prepubertal)**

- **Pubic Hair (PH-1):** Preadolescent stage characterized by a complete absence of terminal pubic hair.
- **Genitalia (G-1):** Preadolescent stage where the testes, scrotum, and penis remain small and infantile in size. The testicular volume is less than 3 mL.

**SMR STAGE 2 (Early Puberty)**

- **Pubic Hair (PH-2):** There is a sparse, scanty growth of long, slightly pigmented hair situated primarily at the base of the penis.
- **Genitalia (G-2):** Characterized by slight enlargement of the testes and penis. The scrotum becomes enlarged, its texture is altered, and it develops a red hue (reddening). The testicular volume reaches approximately 4 mL, or a length of 2.5 cm.

**SMR STAGE 3 (Mid Puberty)**

- **Pubic Hair (PH-3):** The pubic hair becomes darker, starts to curl, increases in amount, and spreads laterally.
- **Genitalia (G-3):** The penis undergoes significant enlargement, primarily in length (elongation of the penile shaft). The testes and scrotum continue to further enlarge. The testicular volume reaches approximately 10 mL (length 3.3 to 4.0 cm).

**SMR STAGE 4 (Late Puberty)**

- **Pubic Hair (PH-4):** The hair resembles the adult type, becoming coarse, curly, and involving a larger area, but the absolute quantity remains less than that of a fully mature adult.
- **Genitalia (G-4):** The penis enlarges further in breadth and girth, accompanied by the distinct development of the glans. The testes grow larger, and the scrotal skin undergoes noticeable darkening. The testicular volume reaches approximately 16 mL (length 4.1 to 4.5 cm).

**SMR STAGE 5 (Adult Stage)**

- **Pubic Hair (PH-5):** Attainment of mature adult distribution in quantity and type, with the hair characteristically spreading to the medial surfaces of the thighs.
- **Genitalia (G-5):** The penis, testes, and scrotum reach their fully mature, adult size and proportions. The adult testicular volume is typically 25 mL (length > 4.5 cm).
