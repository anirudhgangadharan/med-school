### **1. Introduction and Definition**

- **Definition:** PPIUCD stands for Postpartum Intrauterine Contraceptive Device. It involves the insertion of an Intrauterine Contraceptive Device (typically Cu-T 380A) into the uterus shortly after delivery to prevent unintended consecutive pregnancies.
- It is a Long-Acting Reversible Contraception (LARC) method that is safe, highly effective, and a satisfactory method irrespective of the mother's breastfeeding status.

### **2. Timing of Insertion**

Insertion of the PPIUCD can be done at specific time intervals following childbirth:

- **Postplacental Insertion:** Done within 10 minutes of the expulsion of the placenta.
- **Intra-cesarean Insertion:** Placed directly into the uterine cavity during a cesarean section, after the removal of the placenta and before the closure of the uterine incision.
- **Immediate Postpartum:** Inserted within 48 hours of delivery (classified as Medical Eligibility Criteria or MEC-1) before the patient is discharged from the hospital.
- **Extended Postpartum / Interval:** Insertion between 48 hours to 4 weeks postpartum is generally avoided (MEC-3). However, it can be safely inserted anytime after 4 to 6 weeks postpartum (interval insertion, MEC-1).

### **3. Technique and Instruments**

- The procedure requires strict aseptic precautions.
- **Instrument of Choice:** **Kelly's long forceps** is specifically used for postplacental insertion in the puerperal uterus.
- **Advantages of Kelly's Forceps:**
    - Its long length enables the provider to safely place the IUCD high up at the fundus of the enlarged puerperal uterus.
    - Transverse serrations provide a good grip on the IUCD.
    - The absence of a catch on the handle protects the IUCD from being accidentally crushed during the procedure.

### **4. Advantages of PPIUCD**

The PPIUCD programme provides several significant benefits, making it an ideal family planning strategy:

- **High Efficacy and Safety:** It is a safe and highly effective method of contraception.
- **Immediate Protection:** Offers immediate action and long-term contraceptive protection.
- **High Accessibility:** Provides special benefits to women having limited access or no access to postpartum care, solving the issue of patients failing to return for an interval family planning visit.
- **Lactation Friendly:** It has no adverse effects on breastfeeding.
- **Convenience and Cost-Effective:** There is no need for frequent hospital visits, requiring no further costs, leading to high continuation rates and user satisfaction.
- **Reversibility:** Ensures an immediate return to fertility following its removal.

### **5. Disadvantages and Complications**

- **Expulsion Rate:** The major drawback of immediate postpartum insertion is that the expulsion rate is significantly higher compared to interval insertion due to the bulky, contracting puerperal uterus.
- Other general IUCD complications such as abnormal menstrual bleeding, cramp-like pain, missing threads, or a minor risk of pelvic inflammatory disease (PID).

### **6. Contraindications**

PPIUCD should be strictly avoided in the presence of the following conditions:

- Postpartum endometritis or infected abortion in the past 3 months.
- Current acute pelvic infection or sexually transmitted infections (STIs).
- Undiagnosed genital tract bleeding.
- Distortion of the shape of the uterine cavity (e.g., congenital uterine malformations or fibroids).
- Significant maternal immunosuppression or trophoblastic disease.
