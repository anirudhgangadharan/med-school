	### Sumatriptan: Mechanism of Action, Adverse Effects & Contraindications
	
	**Diagnosis:** Migraine is a neurological disorder characterised by severe throbbing headache, often unilateral, accompanied by symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, and sensitivity to light (photophobia) and sound (phonophobia).
	
	#### Mechanism of Action
	
	Sumatriptan is a **selective 5-HT1D/1B receptor agonist**. Its anti-migraine activity is attributed to:
	
	- **Constriction of dilated cranial blood vessels**, particularly arterio-venous shunts in the carotid artery, which express 5-HT1D/1B receptors. Dilatation of these shunts during migraine is thought to divert blood flow away from the brain parenchyma.
	- **Reduced 5-HT release** at these blood vessels, as 5-HT1D/1B receptors function as presynaptic autoreceptors.
	- **Inhibition of inflammatory neuropeptide release** around the affected vessels.
	- Suppression of **extravasation of plasma proteins** across dural vessels.
	- Suppression of **neurogenic inflammation** of cranial vessels.
	
	#### Adverse Effects
	
	Sumatriptan's side effects are generally mild, short-lasting, and dose-related, though more common after subcutaneous injection.
	
	- **Common:**
	    - Tightness in head and chest.
	    - Feeling of heat.
	    - Paresthesias in limbs.
	    - Dizziness, weakness.
	    - Pain at injection site (if subcutaneous).
	    - Slight rise in blood pressure (usually not clinically relevant).
	- **Serious/Infrequent:**
	    - Bradycardia.
	    - **Coronary vasospasm**.
	    - Risk of **myocardial infarction** (MI).
	    - Rare reports of sudden deaths.
	    - Seizures.
	    - Hypersensitivity reactions (rare).
	
	#### Contraindications
	
	Sumatriptan should be avoided in patients with:
	
	- **Ischaemic heart disease**.
	- **Hypertension**.
	- Epilepsy.
	- Hepatic or renal impairment.
	- Pregnancy.
	- **Should not be administered within 24 hours of ergotamine**.
	- Caution is advised with **5-HT reuptake inhibitors** (SSRIs), **MAO inhibitors** (as sumatriptan is metabolised by MAO-A isoenzyme), and **lithium**, due to reported interactions.
	- Patients should be **cautioned not to drive** due to potential dizziness and weakness.
