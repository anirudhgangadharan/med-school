1. Oncogenic viruses and the malignant conditions caused by them
	**Oncogenic Viruses and Associated Malignancies**
	
	- Viruses account for the **majority** of organisms with oncogenic potential. They are responsible for 15% of all human malignancies.
	- Viral oncogenesis is a **complex, multistep process** requiring prolonged time.
	
	**DNA Oncogenic Viruses**
	
	- **Papillomaviridae / Polyomaviridae**:
	    - **Human Papillomaviruses (HPV)**:
	        - **Cervical carcinoma**
	        - Other genital tract carcinomas: **anal, vulval/vaginal, penile**
	        - **Esophageal carcinoma**
	        - **Laryngeal carcinoma**
	        - **Oropharyngeal carcinoma**
	    - **Merkel cell virus**:
	        - **Merkel cell carcinoma of skin**
	- **Herpesviridae**:
	    - **Epstein-Barr virus (EBV)**:
	        - **Burkitt’s lymphoma** (EBV associated with 90% of African and 20% of non-African cases)
	        - **Hodgkin’s disease** (EBV DNA found in Reed-Sternberg cells in ≥50% of cases)
	        - **Nasopharyngeal carcinoma**
	        - **B cell lymphoma** (All CNS non-Hodgkin’s lymphomas and 50% of systemic non-Hodgkin’s lymphomas are EBV DNA or antibody positive)
	        - Post-transplant lymphomas
	    - **Human Herpesvirus-8 (HHV-8)** (also called Kaposi’s sarcoma-associated herpesvirus (KSHV)):
	        - **Kaposi’s sarcoma** (particularly in association with AIDS)
	        - **Castleman’s disease**
	        - **Primary effusion lymphoma** (associated with AIDS)
	- **Hepadnaviridae**:
	    - **Hepatitis B virus (HBV)**:
	        - **Hepatocellular carcinoma** (HBV, in conjunction with HCV, responsible for 70–85% of hepatocellular carcinomas worldwide)
	
	**RNA Oncogenic Viruses**
	
	- **Retroviridae**:
	    - **HTLV-I**:
	        - **Adult T cell leukemia/lymphoma**
	    - **HIV**:
	        - **AIDS-related malignancies** (including Burkitt’s lymphoma, Diffuse large B cell lymphoma, Primary effusion lymphoma, Kaposi’s sarcoma, Invasive cervical carcinoma)
	- **Flaviviridae**:
	    - **Hepatitis C virus (HCV)**:
	        - **Hepatocellular carcinoma** (HCV, in conjunction with HBV, responsible for 70–85% of hepatocellular carcinomas worldwide)
	
	_(Note: Varicella-zoster virus is NOT an oncogenic RNA virus. EBV is NOT associated with carcinoma of the cervix. The association of HSV-2 with cervical cancer and CMV with prostate cancer has not been proved yet. Molluscum contagiosum virus is not oncogenic.)_
