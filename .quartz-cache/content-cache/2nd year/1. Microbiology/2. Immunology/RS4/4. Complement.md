1.  Tri-Dot ELISA Applications
	
	The **Tri-dot test** is a type of **dot blot assay** or **flow-through assay**, which are rapid/simple diagnostic methods. It is a commercially available test format for **antibody detection**.
	
	**Key Application:**
	
	- **HIV TRI-DOT test:** This is a classical example of a flow-through assay.
	    - **Purpose:** It is used to **detect antibodies to HIV-1 and HIV-2 separately** in a patient's serum.
	    - **Principle:**
	        - The test system is typically in a cassette format with a **nitrocellulose membrane (NCM)**.
	        - The NCM is coated at specific regions with **HIV-1 and HIV-2 antigens**, along with a **control region coated with anti-human Ig**.
	        - When a patient's serum (sample) is added, if **HIV antibodies are present, they bind to the immobilized antigens**.
	        - A **Protein-A conjugate** (present in the buffer) then binds to the **Fc portion of these HIV antibodies**, resulting in **distinct pinkish-purple dots** for HIV-1 and/or HIV-2.
	        - A control dot also forms, confirming the test validity.
	    - **Clinical Relevance:** Provides a rapid and distinct qualitative diagnosis for HIV infection by differentiating between HIV-1 and HIV-2 antibodies.
