	**Bacteria Causing Diabetic Foot Infection**
	
	Diabetic foot infections are often polymicrobial and involve a range of bacteria. The most common bacteria isolated include:
	
	- **Gram-positive Cocci:**
	    
	    - _**Staphylococcus aureus**_
	    - **Group B streptococci**
	- **Gram-negative Bacilli:**
	    
	    - **Members of the Enterobacteriaceae**, with _**Escherichia coli**_ specifically noted
	    - _**Pseudomonas aeruginosa**_
	- **Anaerobes:**
	    
	    - E.g., _**Bacteroides fragilis**_
