## Sociodemographic Profile

## 1. Family

## Definition

- Group of individuals related by blood, marriage, or adoption, living together and sharing a common kitchen/household.
    
- **Park’s Textbook:** “Family is a group of persons, usually living together, who are related by birth or marriage or adoption and constitute a single household.”
    

## Types of Families

- **Nuclear family:** Parents + unmarried children.
    
- **Joint family:** Multiple generations + collateral relatives.
    
- **Extended family:** Includes additional kin beyond parents and children.
    

---

## Family Cycle

1. **Formation** – Marriage.
    
2. **Expansion** – Birth/arrival of children.
    
3. **Completion** – Last child born.
    
4. **Contraction** – Children leave/marriage of children.
    
5. **Dissolution** – Death of spouse(s)/members.
