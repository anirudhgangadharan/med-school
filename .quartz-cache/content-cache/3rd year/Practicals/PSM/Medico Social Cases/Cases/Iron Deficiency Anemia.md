### History of Present Illness (HOPI)

Patient was apparently alright 7 days back, then she developed generalized weakness, which was insidious in onset and progressive in nature. The weakness is present throughout the day, affecting her routine activities such as household works and working at farm. It is relieved on taking adequate rest. There is no diurnal variation. She gives history of easy fatigability, back pain and giddiness even with minimal exertion.

Along with weakness, she also complains of headache for the past 7 days, which is insidious in onset and continuous in nature. The pain is described as dull aching, generalized and non-throbbing.

---

## PAGE 2 — HOPI Continued

There is no history of early morning worsening, no projectile vomiting, no visual disturbance, and no loss of consciousness.

The patient sought medical attention at a hospital in Savadatti, Belagavi, where she was investigated and found to have severe anemia with Hb 4 gm/L, following which she was referred and has now presented to KHPIMS, Gadag for further evaluation and management.

On further enquiry, the patient gives a significant menstrual history. She reports a past history of heavy menstrual bleeding for 6–7 days/cycle with passage of clots suggestive of menorrhagia. She states during these cycles, she required frequent change of cloths and experienced excessive blood loss. However, for the past few cycles, her menstruation lasted only for 3 days. There is no h/o amenorrhoea.

### Prevention

**1) Individual Level**

**a) Primary Level**

- Health Promotion — Health education and Nutrition
- Specific Protection — Iron and folate supplements

**b) Secondary Level**

- Early diagnosis and treatment
- Iron therapy — Moderate Anemia
- Blood transfusion — Severe Anemia

**c) Tertiary Level**

- Treatment of Anemia
- Rehabilitation with iron rich food and tablets.
