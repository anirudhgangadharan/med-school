## PAGE 51 — Advice & Prevention

**Advice:**

- Decrease sugar and salt intake
- Consume nutritious food with dietary fibres
- Cut down carbohydrate-rich diet
- Aim for optimal weight and do physical activity
- Take medication regularly
- Visit a physician monthly for RBS checkup

---

**Prevention:**

**Individual Level:**

**1. Primary Prevention:**

a) **Population Strategy:**

- Primordial prevention of emergence of risk factors like obesity, sedentary life style, diet with unsaturated fats, malnutrition, alcohol abuse.
- Maintenance of normal body weight
- Adequate protein intake, reduced sugar intake

b) **High Risk Strategy:**

- Correction of sedentary life style, over nutrition and obesity
- Avoid alcohol, smoking

**2. Secondary Prevention:**

- Early diagnosis and start treatment
- Take small meals frequently
- Anti-diabetic drug adherence
- Routine checkup (RBS, urine protein, BP)
- HbA₁C — 6 months once

**3. Tertiary Prevention:**

- Prevent complications like neuropathy, nephropathy and retinopathy
- Rehabilitation and manage complications
