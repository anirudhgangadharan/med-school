### Primary Prevention

- Health education
- Safe drinking water
- Hand wash before food
- Use of mosquito repellent
- Brandish full sleeve clothes
- Elimination of stagnant water
- Vector control measures
- Source reduction
- Use of larvicides
- Fogging during outbreaks
- Immunisation:
    - Typhoid vaccine, Japanese encephalitis vaccine
    - Chemo prophylaxis when indicated

---

### Secondary Prevention

- Early diagnosis by Widal test, NSI antigen, Malaria smear

---

### Tertiary Prevention

- Manage complications like dehydration, shock, renal failure, encephalopathy with supportive care
- Educate on prevention of recurrence
- Rehabilitation of recovery

---

### Quaternary Prevention

- Avoid unnecessary blood specimen contribution and recurrence

---

### Community Health Measures

- Fogging and antivirus means, strengthen immunisation.
