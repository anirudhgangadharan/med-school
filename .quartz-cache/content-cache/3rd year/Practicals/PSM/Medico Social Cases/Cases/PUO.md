## PAGE 101 — ADVICE, PREVENTION & SUMMARY

### Advice

- Usage of bednets
- Proper waste disposal

---

### Prevention

#### i) Individual Level

**1. Primary Prevention:**

- Health education – awareness about vector breeding sites
- Avoid artificial collection of water
- Usage of bednets
- Usage of mosquito repellants

**2. Secondary Prevention:**

- Early diagnosis and treatment
- Prevention of complications

**3. Tertiary Prevention:**

- Prevention of disability and rehabilitation

---

#### ii) Family Level

- Hygiene and Sanitation
- Detection and estimation of mosquito breeding sites
- Proper covering of water stores
- Following dry day programme

---

#### iii) Community Level

- Providing health education
- Integrated Vector Borne Disease Control Programme
- **NVBDCP**
