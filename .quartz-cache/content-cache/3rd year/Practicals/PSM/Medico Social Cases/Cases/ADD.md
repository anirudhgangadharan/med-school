## PAGE 10 (Image 10) — Medico-Social Diagnosis, Advice & Prevention

### Medico-Social Diagnosis

Here is a 15 month old child resident of Lakkundi belonging to Class II according to Modified B.G. Prasad classification, living in pucca house with moderate environmental condition, is diagnosed with acute diarrhoeal disease with mild dehydration.

### Advice

- Immunize the child regularly
- Wash hands before and after feeding and after defecation
- Feed with calorie rich food items
- Increase frequency of breast feeding

### Prevention

**1. Individual Level**

**i. Primary Prevention**

- a. Health promotion — Mother is advised and educated about proper food, sanitation, hand hygiene
- b. Specific protection — Take routine immunization; Correction of electrolyte imbalance

**ii. Secondary Prevention**

- Early diagnosis, treatment, rehydration
- Treatment with ORS, paracetamol and antibiotics if needed

**iii. Tertiary Prevention**

- Disability prevention and rehabilitation

**2. Family Level**

- Food and hand hygiene to family members
- Use of clean water for drinking and cooking purpose
- Adopt vector control measures

**3. Community Level** _(continued from Page 1)_

- Maternal and child health
- IMNCI
- Integrated global action plan for prevention and control of Pneumonia and Diarrhoea
- Diarrhoeal disease control programme
